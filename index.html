How it works

  1. We set it up

    We come to your shop and connect it to the number your customers already call. It takes about 30 minutes of your time. You read and approve every message before anything goes out.

  2. It catches what you miss

    A missed call gets a text within a minute. Two quick questions sort it: emergencies alert your on-call tech, everything else goes to your calendar.

  3. You see what it brought in

    On the 1st of each month you get a one-page report: calls caught, jobs booked, quotes that need your call. We walk you through it in five minutes.

What it does

Missed-call text-back

Nobody picks up, the caller gets a text from your shop asking what they need.

Booking

Customers pick an open slot by text or from a link on your website and Google listing.

Quote follow-up

Quotes that go quiet get a text on day 2, day 5, and day 10. After that you get a short list to call.

Review requests

Every finished job gets one polite request for a Google review. One, not five.

Emergency sorting

Emergencies reach you or your on-call tech right away, any hour. Routine jobs wait for morning so you can sleep.

Safety rule

Anyone who mentions a gas smell, sparks, or smoke is told to get out and call 911. The system never tries to book that one.

Three things you stay in charge of

How we protect your shop's data

Your customer list is the most valuable thing your shop owns. Here is exactly how we handle it. All of this is written into your service agreement too.

Your accounts, your keys

The texting and scheduling accounts are set up in your shop's name. You give us access, and you can turn it off any time. If you cancel, you keep everything.

We see as little as possible

Every shop starts at counts only. We see dollar figures only if you choose to share them. We never take or store credit card numbers.

Trusted providers only

Messages and bookings run on well-known texting and scheduling providers that encrypt your data while it moves and while it is stored. We don't keep your customer list on our own computers.

Locked-down logins

Every account we use has two-step login, every password lives in a business password manager, and our laptops are fully encrypted. Only the two owners have access. No outside contractors, ever.

Never sold, never shared

We don't sell your data, share it, or mix it with another shop's. The AI tools we use are ones that don't train on your customers' messages.

Gone when you say so

Cancel and we delete our copies within 30 days. Want an export first? We hand it over. If anything ever goes wrong, you hear from us directly, in writing, right away.

Straight answers

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Customers keep calling the number they know. We set that number to pass missed calls to the system, and we check that your phone carrier supports it before we start.

Will my customers know it is automated?

The first text reads like your office wrote it, because you approved the wording. If a customer asks something the system is not sure about, it stops and your office gets the message to answer by hand.

What if it gets something wrong?

It asks customers to answer with a number, so there is little to misread. It never guesses a price and never books a gas, smoke, or sparks call. Anything unclear goes to a person.

What happens to an emergency at 2am?

The customer is told help is on the way and your on-call tech gets a text, or a text and a call if you prefer. If he has not responded in 10 minutes, you get alerted too.

Is it OK to text my customers?

The system texts people who called your shop first, every first message says how to opt out, and we register your business with the phone carriers before sending anything. Anyone who replies STOP is never texted again.

I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Does this replace it?

No. Keep what you have. This sits in front of it and catches the calls and quotes that slip through. Tell us which one you use and we will set it up to work alongside.

Who sees my numbers?

You choose. At the default level we see counts, such as how many calls and jobs, and no dollar figures at all. We never share your data or use it with another shop.

Is my customers' information safe?

Yes. It lives in accounts under your shop's name with trusted providers that encrypt it, only the two owners can get in, and every login uses two-step sign-in. We never sell it or share it, and we delete our copies within 30 days if you leave. The full list is in the "How we protect your shop's data" section.

You are a new company. Why should I trust you?

Fair question. That is why the first two shops pay nothing until they have seen it work, why there is no contract, and why the account stays in your name. If we are not earning our fee, you cancel and keep everything.
